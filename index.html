Anxious

"I find it hard beliening that I will win or wherether I even deserve to, sometimes it just seems logical to rather focus my means towards securing my comfort when I fail"

With women empowerment probably at its all time high, I am tempted to blame my frustrations on a claim that, what began as an innocent stand for women has become a stump on men.

But then again in my life I have withnessed enormous stagnance that knows no gender. A fact only a hypocrite would overlook.

So simply put, I am just one of overwhelmingly many young adults, peicing my life back together from bits of destructive teenage years and unconstructive early twenties. I am screwed and scared that I just might not pull off the unscrewing.

Growth opportunities are scarce, and worse than scarce opportunities is the ambiguity surrounding the promising ones. There is just no certainty on how you should position yourself? With often fear making up most of the uncertainty.

Ofcourse it is easier to risk everything when you are younger and naive because that "everything" makes up most of what you will still have after the fall, especially time.

As you age however, its a different world entirely.
